Threat-Informed Defense & Continuous Validation

Defensive security is not passive listening; it is an active, empirical engineering discipline. My methodology focuses on operationalizing the MITRE ATT&CK framework within continuous threat exposure management (CTEM) loops: constructing high-fidelity detection rules, validating adversary emulation coverage, eliminating alert fatigue through SOAR playbooks, and ensuring incident responders possess instant telemetry to isolate compromises before lateral movement can occur.
